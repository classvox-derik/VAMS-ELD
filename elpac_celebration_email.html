⚠️ Important Reminder

Please be mindful when discussing ELPAC scores with students and parents. Passing the ELPAC test is not the same as being reclassified. Students must still meet all 4 requirements to be officially reclassified as RFEP (Reclassified Fluent English Proficient). Let's celebrate their achievement while keeping this distinction clear.
